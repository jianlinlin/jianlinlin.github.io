Extreme Heat and Old Age

“Extreme heat may have a harsher effect on older people than doctors previously thought.”

节选翻译：极端高温对老年人的影响可能比医生此前认为的更严重。

阅读 Breaking News English 原文

训练阅读与中文翻译

English reading · 改写训练文本

Older adults may be more vulnerable to dangerous heat than once assumed. With age, the body's ability to regulate temperature can weaken. During long heatwaves, reduced sweating, added strain on the heart, and slower expansion of blood vessels may make it harder to release heat. If the body cannot cool itself, the risk of severe illness rises. This risk may be greatest for older adults in low-income countries.

中文翻译

老年人面对危险高温时可能比过去估计的更加脆弱。随着年龄增长，人体调节体温的能力可能减弱。在持续较久的热浪期间，出汗减少、心脏负担加重以及血管扩张变慢，可能使身体更难散热。如果身体无法自行降温，严重患病的风险就会上升。低收入国家的老年人面临的风险可能最高。

重点词汇与单词造句

先看释义和例句，再用每个词或短语独立写一句英文。尽量联系研究、数据或公共健康主题。

vulnerable adj.

脆弱的；易受……影响的。常用搭配：be vulnerable to。

Example: Older adults may be particularly vulnerable to extreme heat.

regulate v.

调节；控制。常用搭配：regulate body temperature。

Example: The body regulates its temperature in several ways.

heatwave n.

热浪；持续高温天气。常用搭配：a prolonged heatwave。

Example: Prolonged heatwaves can increase health risks.

strain n.

压力；负担。常用搭配：place strain on。

Example: High temperatures place additional strain on the heart.

expand v.

扩张；扩大。常用搭配：blood vessels expand。

Example: Blood vessels may expand more slowly with age.

release v.

释放；散发。这里指身体向外散热。

Example: The body releases heat through the skin.

pose a risk phrase

构成风险；带来风险。常用结构：pose a risk to。

Example: Extreme temperatures pose a risk to older adults.

estimate n. / v.

估计；估算。常用搭配：previous estimates。

Example: Previous estimates may understate the effects of heat.

中译英练习

先独立翻译，再展开参考表达。参考答案不止一种，注意时态、搭配和句子结构。

  1. 随着年龄增长，人体调节体温的能力可能减弱。

    参考表达

    With age, the body's ability to regulate temperature can weaken.

  2. 持续较久的热浪可能增加健康风险。

    参考表达

    Prolonged heatwaves may increase health risks.

  3. 高温会给心脏带来额外负担。

    参考表达

    High temperatures place additional strain on the heart.

  4. 如果身体无法自行降温，严重患病的风险就会上升。

    参考表达

    If the body cannot cool itself, the risk of severe illness rises.
