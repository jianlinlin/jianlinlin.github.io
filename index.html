01ABOUT

Curious about
the bigger picture.

I am a PhD student at Macao Polytechnic University. My work brings together epidemiology, longitudinal data, and applied statistics to understand health across the life course.

I’m especially interested in healthy aging, chronic conditions, lifestyle and behavior, and the methods that make observational health research more informative. I enjoy turning complex datasets into clear questions, careful analyses, and useful evidence.
