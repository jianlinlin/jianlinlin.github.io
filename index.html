About

I am a PhD student at Macao Polytechnic University. My research uses epidemiological and statistical approaches to study population health, with a particular interest in aging and changes in health over time. I am interested in careful study design, longitudinal data, and methods that can make evidence from observational health research more informative.

My previous training is in applied statistics and epidemiology and health statistics. I have worked on questions spanning healthy aging, chronic conditions, lifestyle, health services, and clinical research.

Interests

  • Healthy aging and life-course health
  • Longitudinal epidemiology and population cohorts
  • Causal inference and applied statistics
  • Health services, chronic conditions, and lifestyle
  • R programming and reproducible analysis
